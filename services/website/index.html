Denkzeug

[ˈdɛŋkˌtsɔʏ̯k] “DENK-tsoyk” German, noun

A Denkzeug is an object that is not part of the body of a living or artificial organism, used to extend the functions of the mind in order to achieve a distant goal.

A tool for thought. Quite literally.
